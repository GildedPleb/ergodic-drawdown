- **`v32/app.html`** is generated by `v32/build.py` from `../v32/app.html`. The v32 chart rendering, layout and style are untouched; the build only adds the items below.
  1. **SM fonts.** Cormorant at 21px and up becomes SM Cormorant; Cormorant under 21px becomes SM EB Garamond; Inter and JetBrains Mono become SM Jost. One metric pin: `.kick{line-height:12px}`. Jost's "normal" line box is 3px taller than Inter's, which would have pushed the chart down 3px.
  2. **₿ prefix,** applied at draw time to canvas text and DOM text: ₿13.1, your ₿5.2, below ₿1 · danger, axis ₿0–₿20.
  3. **Dock row:** search far left, then MODELS · PLANS · STATS (Plans default), then the gear far right, all on one baseline. The row stays 30px high, the same as v32. While searching, the v32 search row is unchanged.
  4. **World layer:** a separate transparent canvas holding ₿ price (focused fan), $100k in S&P (dotted) and "$100k buys" (wine dash) on a $ log axis.
     - It uses v32's own L/R and `lx`, so the time axis is identical.
     - With Plans selected it sits at 7% opacity, 94% height, 0.6px blur, `aria-hidden`.
     - With Models selected, a push-back transition swaps the layers: 320ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1), the incoming layer delayed 40ms. With reduced motion it becomes 120ms opacity only.
     - A polite live region announces the swap. Keys M and P also swap.
  5. The canvas '⚠' warning glyph is drawn as a filled triangle path. No SM font has ⚠, so it would otherwise fall back to a system font.

# (superseded) StackMath · Depth: two charts, one stage (v43)

**Direction (from the user):** the **drawdown is the main event** and the default view, showing plan fans only, calmly. Bitcoin price, S&P and inflation are modelled but secondary. They live on a **second layer behind** the drawdown and come forward only when **Models** is selected; selecting **Plans** brings the drawdown forward again. This returns to the original StackMath idea of two charts: projected ₿ price and the drawdown.
This **supersedes** `chart/CHART.md`: options A, B and C and the inflation strip, band and Models-only placements.

Pages come from `depth.html?p=1..4` and are rendered with `shoot.js`, using SM fonts only (checked: no fallback). The prototype is `proto.html`.

## The two layers
- **Drawdown (front by default).** Orchid (focused fan) and Coral (context fan) in ₿ on a linear scale, so zero is real. End labels give the median and the p5–p95 range (₿3.9 · ₿0–8.4).
- **World (behind by default).** On one $ log axis: ₿ price (focused fan), "$100k in S&P" (dotted, context) and "$100k today buys" (wine dash, context). Using dollars for all three keeps inflation on the same axis without a band or a separate strip.
- **Back state:** 7% opacity, 94% height (scaled from the baseline), 0.6px blur, and no hit targets except "tap the whisper to swap". It has `aria-hidden="true"` and `inert`.
- **Time axis is locked.** Both layers share the same x scale and plot edges (left 44px and right 150px on desktop; 34/92 on phone). Depth only ever scales vertically from the baseline, so a year never drifts sideways during or after a swap.
- **Stats always reads the drawdown layer** ("READS: DRAWDOWN" tag). Stats answers "what does my plan make", whichever layer is in front. World readouts (₿ price at 68, "$1 → $0.41") live in the Models rows.
- **Desktop:** all three columns are visible, so the "selected" tab is whichever column you last touched. Touching a Models row brings the world forward; touching a Plans row or a Stats tile brings the drawdown back.

## Transitions (≤400ms each)
| | Treatment | Motion | Reduced motion | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Push back**: the front layer scales to 94% height from the baseline, dims to 7% and blurs 0.6px; the back layer rises to 100% | 320ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1); the incoming layer starts 40ms late | opacity only, 120ms linear; no scale or blur | **Recommended** |
| 2 | **Cross-fade**: opacity only | 240ms linear | instant | Calmest, but nothing says "behind", so the whisper reads as noise |
| 3 | **Stacked sheet**: the front slides down 24px like a card; the back lifts 8px | 360ms ease-out | instant | Strong metaphor, but it moves the time axis vertically mid-flight and competes with the drawer drag |
| — | Card flip | — | — | Rejected: axis alignment breaks for half the move; too theatrical |

**Why push back:** it shows the layer model honestly, as one chart stepping behind another. It keeps the years exactly aligned, and it is cheap to render (GPU transform, opacity and filter).

**Accessibility (all treatments):**
- Only the active chart is in the accessibility tree; the back layer is `aria-hidden` and `inert`.
- Each layer has `role="img"` with a plain-words label.
- The tabs are a real tablist.
- A polite live region announces "Showing the world: bitcoin price, S&P and inflation." or "Showing your plan."
- Focus stays on the tab that was pressed.
- The prototype also accepts keys M and P.

**Prototype (verified headless):** clicking Models swaps the classes and the aria-hidden and inert attributes and updates the live text, with no console errors. Pressing P swaps back. With reduced motion, the transition is 0.12s and opacity-only.
