# K5: default-size hover (V32-BUILD:864)

Grading notes for Probe. Default size only (11px mark, 1px stroke, 2px gap, 5px core).

- **Hover** (fine pointer, enabled parent): a second outline, 1px solid `currentColor`, at
  `--s-0` (2px) from the mark. The mark's colour is its parent's: `--accent` on the Clamp tick,
  the asset's path colour on a key. The core does not change.
- **Focus** stays outside it: the key's ring is `--focus-w` (2px) `--focus` at `--s-1` (4px),
  so a 1px gap separates it from the hover ring.
- **Disabled**: a disabled tick (`disabled`) and an inert key (`aria-disabled`) fall outside
  `:enabled:not([aria-disabled]):hover`, so they get no ring.
- **Contrast** (`test/v2/contrast.test.ts`): every mark colour (3 asset paths, 8 swatches,
  `--accent`) is at least 3:1 on `--surface` and on `--ground` in both themes. Lowest: plan-1 on
  linen surface 3.55, sp on linen surface 3.68, plan-4 and plan-6 on linen surface 3.85,
  plan-1 on linen ground 3.90, inflation on dark surface 3.94.
- **Baselines** (`k5-hover.pw.ts`, Chromium 390 and 1440, dark and linen):
  `k5--{tick,key}-hover[-on]--<theme>.png`, `k5--key-focus-hover--<theme>.png`.
